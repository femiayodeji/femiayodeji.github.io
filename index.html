About

Curious by nature,
precise by practice.

I'm a Senior Software Engineer based in Ibadan, Nigeria, with 8+ years building reliable backend architectures across FinTech, E-commerce, EdTech, and the public sector — event-driven microservices and high-throughput transaction flows sustaining 100K+ MAU across African and global markets.

For the last three years my work has centred on agentic AI — multi-agent orchestration, embedding and LLM pipelines, and production platforms where model output has to be correct, observable, and cheap enough to run.

Underneath all of it: system correctness, reliability, and performance — distributed locking, idempotency, latency-tuned databases, and observability that surfaces what production is actually doing.

Outside engineering, I founded Alterbin & W.A.R.M Foundation — circular economy and sustainability education across Nigerian schools and communities. I also paint, study human anatomy, and swim — some problems only solve themselves underwater.
