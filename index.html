About

I'm a Senior Backend Engineer and AI Solutions Architect based in Ibadan, Nigeria. Over six years, I've led engineering teams, built payment pipelines processing millions in transactions, scaled platforms to 100K+ monthly active users, and shipped production AI systems across FinTech, EdTech, E-commerce, and Gaming.

I'm equally at home architecting microservices on AWS, integrating LLMs into real products, and leading cross-functional teams — from database design through to production deployment.

Beyond the terminal, I'm a web game developer who builds educational games that make learning feel like play. I'm also the founder of Alterbin & W.A.R.M Foundation, running circular economy systems and sustainability education across Nigerian schools.

I find deep inspiration in biology — the logic of living systems maps beautifully onto distributed architecture. I paint when I need to slow down. I swim when I need to think.

Sustainability

Giving things
new stories.

In 2023 I founded Alterbin and the W.A.R.M Foundation (Waste-As-Raw-Material) — a circular economy ecosystem combining waste recovery, reuse logistics, and sustainability education right here in Ibadan.

We've recovered and traded 3,000+ kg of PET plastic, built the Recyschool programme enabling students to create products from ≥80% waste materials, and provided real educational support — textbooks and exam-fee sponsorship — for 7 students across 2 schools.

Circular economy isn't just about recycling. It's a design philosophy. I apply it to code too — every legacy system I touch gets a new story.
